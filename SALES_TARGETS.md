# Sales Targets — Optimus Testing (TestFlow)

A starting list of companies that fit the buyer profile. Sized for India first because the entire schema, template library, and language defaults assume Indian standards (BIS/IS). International expansion is post-product-market-fit.

Buyer profile (the person you actually pitch to):
- **Title:** Head of Testing & Commissioning, Project Manager (Substations), Commissioning Manager
- **Pain:** Excel-and-WhatsApp coordination across multiple sites, paper test sheets, slow client handover, no audit trail when something goes wrong
- **Budget authority:** ₹2–10 lakh/year for "Team" tier; enterprise tier negotiated per contract

## International (post Indian PMF — 12–18 months out)

Same buyer profile, different markets:
- **Middle East:** ADWEA (UAE), Saudi Electricity Company, Q-Energy (Qatar) — heavy substation buildout, English-speaking
- **South-East Asia:** PLN (Indonesia), Tenaga Nasional (Malaysia), EVN (Vietnam) — growing grids
- **Africa:** Eskom (South Africa), TANESCO (Tanzania), KPLC (Kenya) — donor-funded substation projects
- **UK & Europe:** very different standards (IEC/BS, not IS). Significant template re-engineering required.

Don't pursue these in year 1. Each requires a localized template library and local sales presence.

## What makes this "worth selling"

For internal positioning when you talk to investors / partners — the math behind why this is a real business:

**Market size**:
- India: ~5,000+ substations commissioned per year (utilities + private + industrial + renewable)
- ~500 active commissioning contractor companies
- Average buyer pays ₹5L/year on Team tier × 500 companies = **₹25 Cr (~$3M) ARR ceiling in India** before any pricing power
- 10× that internationally → meaningful but not unicorn-scale TAM

**Wedge advantage**:
- No incumbent owns this space digitally. Most contractors still use Excel templates.
- High switching cost once installed (template library, historical records, mobile habits)
- Multi-tenant architecture means platform cost scales sub-linearly with customers

**Risks** (be honest with yourself):
- Procurement cycles at PSUs are 12–18 months
- Buyers are technical, not commercial — won't tolerate buggy UX
- Language: shop floor often Hindi/regional. English-only UI is a real ceiling.
- Pricing power is limited; this isn't SAP — buyers will resist > ₹10L/year contracts

**Why ship now anyway**:
- The 46-template library + role workflow + mobile app + AI report combo doesn't exist commercially
- Indian commissioning capacity is doubling by 2030 (national grid plan) — the wave is coming
- Even 50 paying customers @ ₹4L/year = ₹2 Cr ARR — that's a real business
